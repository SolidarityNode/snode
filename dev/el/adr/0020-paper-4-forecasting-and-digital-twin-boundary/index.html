    0020. Boundary between Paper 4 forecasting and a future digital twin¶

    • Status: Proposed (stub, needs a human decision)
    • Date: 2026-11-26
    • Deciders:
    • Related: AGENTS.md invariant 8, OQ-23, OQ-31, OQ-32, OQ-33, OQ-34, OQ-40; ADR 0006, 0017

    Context¶

    AGENTS.md invariant 8: the Digital Twin (ψηφιακό δίδυμο) is a future direction, not an assumption; do not build twin-specific machinery yet. OQ-23 keeps the wording consistent (Paper 1's conclusion speaks of a "probabilistic baseline Digital Twin").

    The maintainer decided (2026-11-26) that Paper 4 includes forecasting and (near) real-time maps (OQ-31 to OQ-34): a current picture, a nowcast by DBN filtering and a forecast by DBN prediction with external signals; risk maps from hazard forecasts; a weekly and an hourly time scale with a hand-over between them; one-month and conditional forecasts. Each update is a scheduled, reproducible run in the worker, not a continuous stream, and forecasts support decisions without acting automatically (invariant 7).

    These features come close to what is often called a digital twin, so the line must be stated before Paper 4 is specified, or tickets will drift into twin machinery.

    Questions to answer¶

    • Which of the following are inside Paper 4 and which stay for later:
    • scheduled nowcast and forecast runs at fixed intervals (decided inside: OQ-31);
    • ingestion of external hazard forecasts and warnings (OQ-40);
    • automatic start of crisis mode by a warning (OQ-31 says "by a warning or by hand");
    • continuous streaming updates, event-driven state synchronisation, bidirectional coupling with operational systems, automatic actions (OQ-31 rejects streaming and automatic action);
    • a persistent "live" state of the territory kept between runs, as opposed to state reconstructed by each run from recorded observations.
    • How the wording of Papers 1 and 4 refers to the digital twin (OQ-23).
    • Which platform pieces these need (scheduled jobs: ADR 0006 trigger 3; result retention for frequent crisis runs: ADR 0017), and what must not be built yet.

    Options¶

    1. Forecasting as scheduled runs only (the OQ-31 decision): every map is a normal run with recorded inputs; no persistent live state, no streaming, no actions. A digital twin, if ever, is a separate future ADR.
    2. Option 1 plus a persistent nowcast state carried between runs, still without streaming or actions.
    3. Treat Paper 4 as a first digital twin (live state, streaming inputs): contradicts invariant 8 and OQ-31 as decided.

    Decision¶

    Not decided. To be taken by the maintainer before the Paper 4 spec (04-resilience.md) is written.

    Consequences¶

    To be written with the decision: which Paper 4 tickets are allowed, what is explicitly out of scope, and what would make us revisit invariant 8.
